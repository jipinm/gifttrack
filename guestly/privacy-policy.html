Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data Guestly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Overview

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Guestly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Guestly mobile application and related services. By using Guestly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Effective: February 14, 2026 Last Updated: February 14, 2026 jipinm@gmail.com
Data Collection

What Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to deliver and improve the Guestly experience.

Account Information

Basic details you provide when you create or manage your account.

  • Name and display name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • State/Province
  • District
  • City

Event & Guest Data

Information you enter while managing events and guest lists inside the app.

  • Event names, dates & venues
  • Guest names & contact details
  • RSVP and attendance status
  • Gift records and notes

Device & Usage Data

Technical information collected automatically to maintain app performance.

  • Device type and OS version
  • App version and crash reports
  • Feature usage patterns
  • IP address (anonymised)
Data Usage

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to operate, improve, and support the Guestly app.

Providing Core Services

We use your account and event data to deliver the core features of Guestly — creating and managing guest lists, scheduling events, tracking RSVPs and gifts, and syncing your data across your devices.

Improving the App

Aggregated and anonymised usage data helps us understand which features are most valuable, identify bugs, and prioritise improvements. We do not use personal data for this purpose without your consent.

Notifications & Reminders

If you enable push notifications, we use your event data to send you timely reminders about upcoming events and guest activity. You can manage notification preferences at any time in your device settings.

Customer Support

When you contact us for support via email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may use relevant account information to diagnose and resolve your issue. Support communications are retained only as long as necessary.

Security & Fraud Prevention

We use technical and account data to detect suspicious activity, prevent unauthorised access, and keep your account secure. This processing is essential to the safety of all users.

Data Sharing

Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the limited circumstances described below.

Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers for cloud hosting, analytics, and crash reporting. These partners process data only on our behalf and under strict confidentiality agreements. They are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Legal Obligations

We may disclose your information when required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority. We will notify you where legally permissible before making such a disclosure.

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity. We will provide notice before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

Security & Retention

Keeping Your Data Safe

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

Encryption

All data transmitted between the Guestly app and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security). Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, unless we are required to retain it for legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

Guestly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us at jipinm@gmail.com.

International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Your Rights

Control Over Your Data

You have meaningful rights over the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at any time to exercise them.

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Correction

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Deletion

Request the deletion of your personal data (right to erasure).

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Objection

Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.

Restriction

Request that we restrict how we process your data.

Questions About This Policy?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, our team is happy to help.
